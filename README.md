# Uttarakhand-Agriculture-Market-Dashboard-
*Introduction-->
Agriculture plays a vital role in Uttarakhand’s economy, with multiple districts contributing to the production and distribution of agricultural commodities. Large volumes of market data are generated daily, including pricing, commodity types, and market activity. However, this data is often fragmented and difficult to interpret without proper analytical tools.
This project presents a Business Intelligence solution that transforms raw agricultural data into meaningful insights using interactive dashboards. The system enables stakeholders to analyze price trends, identify high-performing markets, and evaluate risk through volatility analysis.

*Problem Statement-->
1)Agricultural market data across Uttarakhand is:
2)Distributed across multiple districts and markets
3)Complex and unstructured
4)Difficult to analyze manually
This leads to challenges such as:
1)nability to track long-term price trends
2)Difficulty in identifying profitable markets
3)Lack of insights into price volatility
4)Limited data-driven decision-making

*Objectives-->
The key objectives of this project are:
1)To consolidate multi-district agricultural data into a unified dataset
2)To develop an interactive dashboard for data exploration
3)To analyze price trends, market activity, and commodity diversity
4)To provide insights for farmers, traders, and policymakers

*Data Processing and Preparation-->
1)To ensure data quality and usability, the following steps were performed:
2)Cleaned inconsistent and duplicate records
3)Standardized district, market, and commodity names
4)Handled missing values
5)Converted date fields for time-series analysis
6)Created derived features:
   Price Volatility = Max Price – Min Price
   Year and Month columns

*Methodology-->
The project follows a structured approach:
1)Data Collection – Obtained from government dataset
2)Data Cleaning & Transformation – Using Power Query
3)Data Modeling – Establishing relationships between tables
4)Analysis using DAX – Creating calculated measures and KPIs
5)Visualization – Building interactive dashboard in Power BI



*Dashboard Design and Key Visuals-->
The dashboard consists of multiple visual components:
1) Price Trend Analysis:
     Displays year-wise trends of minimum, maximum, and modal prices
     Helps identify long-term price movement
2) District-wise Analysis:
     Map visualization showing commodity distribution
     Highlights regional differences
3) Price Comparison:
     Bar charts comparing district-level price metrics
     Identifies premium and low-value districts
4) Market Contribution:
     Treemap showing contribution of different markets
     Highlights dominant markets like Haldwani
5) Price Volatility Analysis:
     Funnel chart ranking districts based on price fluctuations
     Helps identify stable vs risky markets
6) Market Competitiveness:
    Bubble chart combining:
      Price
      Commodity diversity
      Market activity

*Business Impact and Insights-->
The dashboard provides several key insights:
1)Udham Singh Nagar identified as the most active trading district
2)Nainital shows higher average pricing (premium market)
3)Haldwani contributes the highest market value
4)Dehradun exhibits the highest price volatility
5)Overall increase in modal prices over time, indicating improved market conditions
These insights help in:
1)Selecting profitable markets
2)Managing price risk
3)Making informed agricultural decisions

*Results and Outcomes-->
1)Successfully analyzed 6+ lakh records
2)Converted raw data into meaningful insights
3)Built a scalable and interactive BI solution
4)Improved accessibility of agricultural data

*Future Scope-->
1)Integration of predictive models (ARIMA, Prophet)
2)Inclusion of weather and economic data
3)Development of real-time dashboards
4)Advanced analytics for profit optimization

*Conclusion-->
This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence tools like Power BI can transform complex agricultural datasets into actionable insights. By combining data engineering, analytics, and visualization, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Uttarakhand’s agricultural market system and supports data-driven decision-making.
